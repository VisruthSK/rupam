# Categorical models

Tables over a domain d form a category whose morphisms are domain maps.
Filters are monomorphisms, grouping is a projection onto a key set, and
grouped reductions are key-indexed families pulled back along the projection
to align with observations. Optional values are the Maybe monad, and lifting
is its functorial action. This model guides intuition until a denotational
semantics is needed to justify optimiser rewrites.
